Character: Frodo Baggins
Title: Ring-bearer
Race: Hobbit
Home: The Shire
Items: The One Ring, Sting
Basic Description: An unusually adventurous hobbit tasked with the impossible: to take the One Ring to Mount Doom, the only place where it can be destroyed.




Character: Samwise Gamgee
Title: Gardener
Race: Hobbit
Home: The Shire
Items: Potatoes, Soil from Galadriel’s garden and a Mallorn seed
Basic Description: A loyal hobbit who is initially Frodo’s gardener before he joins and helps Frodo through his journey to Mount Doom to destroy the One Ring.



Character: Meriadoc “Merry” Brandybuck
Race: Hobbit
Home: The Shire
Items: Ent-draught, Barrow Blade
Basic Description: A dependable hobbit who, because of being cousins and friends with the Ring-bearer Frodo Baggins, joins the fellowship with his best friend and cousin Pippin.



Character: Peregrin “Pippin” Took
Race: Hobbit
Home: The Shire
Items: Ent-draught, Barrow Blade
Basic Description: An impulsive, but loyal hobbit who, because of being cousins and friends with the Ring-bearer Frodo Baggins, joins the fellowship with his best friend and cousin Merry.


Character: Gandalf the Grey
Title: Grey Wizard
Race: Maia
Home: Valinor
Items: Wizard Staff, fireworks, Glamdring
Basic Description: The most loyal of the five Wizards sent to Middle-earth, Gandalf discovers that Bilbo and later Frodo possesses the One Ring and initiates the forming of the Fellowship of the Ring.



Character: Aragorn, son of Arathorn
Title: Isildur’s Heir
Race: Human
Home: Rivendell
Items: The Shards of Narsil, Evenstar
Basic Description: The Heir of Isildur, a Ranger of the North, and the rightful heir of Gondor, Aragorn joins the Fellowship of the Ring to help Frodo destroy the One Ring and fiercely fights against the forces of Sauron.



Character: Sauron
Title: The Dark Lord
Race: Maia
Home: Mordor
Items: The One Ring
Basic Description: The Dark Lord, maker of the One Ring.



Character: Legolas Greenleaf
Title: Prince of the Woodland Realm
Race: Elf
Home: Woodland Realm, Mirkwood
Items: Elven Bow, long white knife
Basic Description: A Woodland Elf who forms an unlikely (because of the ancient quarrel between Elves and Dwarves) friendship with Dwarf Gimli and joins the Fellowship of the Ring as a representative of the Elves.



Character: Gimli, son of Glóin
Race: Dwarf
Home: Ered Luin
Items: Axe
Basic Description: A Dwarf of the House of Durin who forms an unlikely (because of the ancient quarrel between Elves and Dwarves) friendship with Elf Legolas and joins the Fellowship of the Ring as a representative of the Dwarves.



Character: Gollum (Sméagol)
Race: Hobbit
Home: Misty Mountains
Items: The One Ring
Basic Description: A Hobbit corrupted by the One Ring, whose only goal after Bilbo took the Ring is to get the precious Ring back into his possession.



Character: Boromir
Title: Captain of the White Tower
Race: Human
Home: Gondor
Items: Great Horn
Basic Description: A renowned warrior and the heir to the Stewardship of Gondor, the first line of defense against Mordor, who joins the Fellowship of the Ring as a representative of Gondor.



Character: Saruman the White
Title: White Wizard, head of the White Council
Race: Maia
Home: Isengard
Items: Wizard Staff, Palantir
Basic Description: The first wizard to be sent to Middle-earth and the head of the White Council, Saruman rules Isengard and studies the movements of Sauron.
